**Culture Manifesto**

Variant Group is a team of self-directed experts. Your place on the Variant Group team means your skills are valued, you are great to work with, and you do not need micromanagement or superficial corporate distractions to do amazing work. 

**How we work:**

Variant Group is a ‘no-nonsense’ organization. We prioritize results, simplicity, and balance. This means avoiding anything that may overcomplicate or interfere with our goals. We are constantly questioning everything and learning more. Our lightweight culture allows everyone to work in the way they prefer. We are collaborative and hold high expectations for one another. Through this teamwork, we ensure everyone delivers great work on time. 

Instead of a large HR department, you are your own HR manager. You have both the freedom and responsibility to advocate for what you need to work at your best. For example, would a subscription to a particular software/service make you more efficient? Do you prefer a certain feedback style from your manager? 

**Our core values:**

**Results:** Collectively, we work on clear-cut deliverables that contribute directly to the overall company goals: (1) acquire more users and (2) increase our average value per user (VPU). Individually, we improve our craft through continuous learning.

**Simplicity:** We keep our goals, workflows, and communication simple.

**Balance:** When we live well, we work well. Our lightweight culture and flexibility allow each team member to thrive both personally and professionally.

We want to keep out of each other’s way, so we can impress ourselves and our teams with excellent work. We value open communication and welcome feedback. Discuss with your manager if there is anything you need to make this a reality. 

We hire very carefully. So long as every team member delivers on expectations, they will ***always*** have a place here at Variant Group.
